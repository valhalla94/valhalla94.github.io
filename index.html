Core Features

Everything you need
for precision dosing

CalcolaBolo goes beyond standard carb counting with clinically-grounded algorithms designed for the real-world complexity of diabetes management.

🧬

Warsaw Formula & FPU

Calculate bolus not just for carbohydrates, but also for fat and protein using the clinically-recognized Warsaw Formula. Handles the "pizza effect" — delayed glucose rise from high-fat, high-protein meals.

📊

Glucose Trend Projection

5 CGM-style trend arrows (falling fast to rising fast) with mg/dL offsets. Combine with live glucose and IOB for informed, adaptive dosing decisions.

⏰

Smart Injection Reminders

Exact-alarm notifications for every fractioned injection in your extended bolus plan — up to 8 hours. Pierces Do Not Disturb, with Done and Snooze actions.

📱

Nightscout CGM Integration

Pull live glucose readings and trend data directly from your Nightscout site. One tap to fill — no manual glucose typing needed.

📷

Barcode Food Scanner

Scan any product barcode with ML Kit and instantly get carbs, fat, and protein from the OpenFoodFacts database. No API key needed.

🕐

Time-of-Day Profiles

Configure different insulin sensitivity factors, carb ratios, and target glucose for different hours. Your body changes — your settings should too.

☁️

Cloud Sync & Offline

Sign in with Google and sync your medical profile to Firebase. Works fully offline and syncs automatically when you're back online.

🎯

Clinician-Configurable

Adjustable FPU percentage, DIA, dose rounding (pen 0.5U or pump 0.1U), max bolus caps — all parameters your diabetologist can tune for you.

How It Works

Four steps to a
precise bolus plan

From meal data to injection plan in seconds. Designed for speed and safety.

1

Enter Meal Data

Input carbs, fat, and protein — manually, from a barcode scan, or pull glucose from Nightscout CGM

2

Set Context

Select your glucose trend arrow, FPU percentage prescribed by your doctor, and injection method (pen or pump)

3

Calculate

The engine computes your immediate bolus (carbs + correction − IOB) and extended bolus plan with fractionated doses

4

Follow the Plan

Schedule alarms for each injection. Receive reminders even through Do Not Disturb — never miss a fractioned dose

Safety First

Built with clinical
caution in mind

Medical calculation tools must be safe. CalcolaBolo includes multiple guardrails to help you avoid dosing errors.

⚠️

Hypo Warning

If blood glucose is below your safety threshold, the app recommends treating hypoglycemia with fast-acting carbs before calculating a bolus.

🛡️

Max Bolus Cap

A visible warning appears when the total suggested dose exceeds your configured maximum — but never blocks you from proceeding.

💡

Single Bolus Advice

When the extended portion is very small (≤2.0 U), the app recommends a single normal bolus — simpler and safer than unnecessary fractioning.

📋

Medical Disclaimer

Explicit disclaimer must be accepted before first use. Designed as a decision-support tool — always consult your diabetologist.
