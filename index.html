Precision Insulin Bolus Calculator

Standard bolus calculators only look at carbs. But high-fat, high-protein meals cause delayed glucose spikes — the "pizza effect." CalcolaBolo uses the Warsaw Formula and Fat-Protein Units (FPU) to compute an immediate bolus plus a fractionated extended bolus with scheduled injection reminders.

Features

Everything you need for precision dosing

CalcolaBolo goes beyond carb counting. Eight powerful features designed with endocrinologists and real-world diabetes management in mind.

📐

Warsaw Formula & FPU

Calculates bolus for carbs, fat, and protein — not just carbs. Handles the "pizza effect" with fractionated extended delivery over 3–8 hours.

📈

CGM-style Trend Projection

Five-arrow trend display (↓↓ rapid fall, ↓ falling, → stable, ↑ rising, ↑↑ rapid rise) with mg/dL offset for glucose-aware dosing decisions.

⏰

Smart Injection Reminders

Exact alarms for each fractionated dose of the extended bolus, up to 8 hours. Pierces Do Not Disturb. Actions: Done / Snooze.

🔗

Nightscout CGM Integration

Real-time glucose reading and trend from your Nightscout site. One-tap to populate — no manual typing. Works with Dexcom, Libre, and Eversense.

📸

Food Barcode Scanner

Scan barcodes with ML Kit and get carbs, fat, and protein from the OpenFoodFacts database. Free, no API key required.

🕐

Time-of-Day Profiles

Different insulin sensitivity factors, I:C ratios, and glucose targets for different hours. Your body changes — your parameters should too.

☁️

Cloud Sync & Offline

Google Sign-In, medical profile sync via Firebase Firestore. Works offline, syncs when you're back online. Your data stays yours.

⚙️

Clinician-configurable Parameters

FPU percentage, DIA, dose rounding (pen 0.5 U or micro 0.1 U), max bolus cap. Everything adjustable under medical supervision.

How It Works

Four simple steps to a safer meal

From meal data to a complete insulin plan in under 30 seconds.

1

Enter your meal data

Type carbs, fat, and protein manually, scan a barcode, or pull glucose from your CGM with one tap.

2

Set the context

Select your glucose trend arrow, FPU percentage, and delivery method (pen or pump) for personalized calculation.

3

Calculate

The Warsaw engine computes your immediate bolus plus a fractionated extended bolus plan with exact dose timing.

4

Follow the plan

Receive scheduled alarms for each injection. Mark doses as done or snooze — the app keeps you on track for up to 8 hours.

Safety First

Built-in guardrails for peace of mind

Every calculation is checked against configurable safety thresholds before a dose is ever suggested.

🛡️

Hypo Warning

If your glucose is below your configured threshold, CalcolaBolo flags it and suggests treating the hypo before calculating a meal bolus.

🔒

Max Bolus Cap

A visible warning appears when the calculated dose exceeds your clinician-set maximum. The app never auto-administers — you always make the final call.

💡

Single Bolus Advice

When the extended portion is ≤ 2.0 units, the app recommends a single immediate injection instead of an unnecessary split. Simpler, safer.

⚕️

Medical Disclaimer

A mandatory disclaimer on first launch ensures every user understands CalcolaBolo is a decision-support tool — not a replacement for clinical judgment.
